Info

Lingkaran is a cultural space housed within a former timber factory in Ipoh, Malaysia. Previously operating as a sawmill, the site now supports exhibitions, public programming, and community use under a shared identity.

Led the art direction to unify independent initiatives into a cohesive platform, including naming, brand identity, and wayfinding. Designed the identity system, website, spatial navigation, and graphic applications across the site. The system remains flexible, adapting with each program in collaboration with artists and collectives.

Web Design

Designed the website as a digital extension of a former timber factory, translating the physical space into a navigable interface. The platform was translated into four languages to reflect Malaysia's major language groups, ensuring accessibility across the communities the space engages.

Logo Transition Behavior

The logo animation operates as both identity and navigation, translating Lingkaran's circular motif and underlying spiral concept into the core interaction framework of the website.

Graphics & Communications

Design the visual identity and graphic production, including event posters, social content, and print for @lingkaranipoh, alongside managing the technical setup that makes performances possible: lighting, staging, and custom builds.
